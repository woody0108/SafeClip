## Why It Matters

This proposal is the current business and product source for SafeClip.
The Android MVP should follow the proposal's recommendation: build the blackbox SD card / USB-C reader submission flow first, and treat phone-mounted recording as a later optional experiment.

## Core Recommendation

Prioritize option 1:

- blackbox SD card / reader integration
- Android-first development
- file selection and automatic candidate search
- video preview
- company review request
- manual or semi-manual report preparation
- structured data collection for future AI

Defer option 2:

- phone-mounted recording
- continuous background camera
- navigation/music coexistence
- Bluetooth or vehicle button triggering

## MVP From Proposal

Required user app functions:

- SD card folder selection
- recent video sorting
- video preview
- report review request
- separate traffic-risk data-use consent
- submission history and status

1.5 or later:

- automatic front/rear pairing
- brand-specific folder matching
- premium reports
- payment and ad integration

## Business Direction

Possible revenue streams:

- free version with ads before submission
- ad removal subscription
- premium report subscription
- USB-C microSD reader package
- B2B traffic-risk data reports

These are not part of MVP 1 implementation.

## Legal And Operating Direction

Initial operation should prefer:

- company reviews submitted video,
- company prepares report material,
- user performs final public-agency submission.

Company-side direct reporting or paid reporting agency behavior requires legal review and possible qualified partner involvement.

## AI Direction

AI is a future review assistant, not MVP 1.

Potential stages:

- key frame extraction,
- number plate OCR candidate,
- date/time/location organization,
- violation type candidate,
- reportability score,
- traffic-risk data automation.

The admin review workflow should eventually produce structured labels that can train or evaluate AI tools.
